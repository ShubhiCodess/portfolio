about_me.md

I wasn’t the kind of kid who could leave things as they were. If something looked boring, felt clunky, or made no sense, I’d end up redesigning it in my head before I even realized it.

What started with sketching, painting, and editing random visuals slowly turned into an obsession with design, technology, and how people interact with both. While studying Engineering, I found myself equally pulled toward coding, designing and building experiences that actually feel human.

Before I knew it, I was leading creative teams, designing for large-scale college events, building full fledged projects, and spending unhealthy amounts of time perfecting tiny details most people would never notice.

Because honestly, the tiny details are usually the difference between something functional and something memorable.

These days, I work somewhere between engineering logic and creative instinct — building projects that balance aesthetics, usability, and purpose. From designing interfaces and visuals to developing hardware based systems, I enjoy turning ideas into things people can see, feel, and connect with.

At the end of the day, I just like building things that don’t feel lifeless. Clean design, thoughtful systems, good storytelling, and a little personality ~ this combination never gets old.
